The goal is to design a tokenomic system such that resources and shelter are more equitably distributed.

Currently, it only exists as some disjoint use cases, specifically concerning the Black token.

Tokens generally represent time spent. There are 13 axes along which a person can garner time.

One of the axes is segment of society, which is black people for the Black Team. People who are "provably black" will get a 100-hour token allotment every day. Blackness will be proven via attestations from other Black people.

The ethnicity of a person does not necessarily dictate their eligibility, anyone can be a Black person so long as at least 12 existing Black people attest that the person belongs & no more than 25% of the positive attestations reject the candidate.
