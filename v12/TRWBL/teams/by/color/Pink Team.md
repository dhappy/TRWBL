## Concierges 

Pink is the first point of contact and triage for new user interaction cases. There are textual, oral, & visual mechanisms of triage. Pink players will be paid to hang out places waiting for potential customers.

In situations where it is suspected that violence or force is to be used, Silver and Orange will be close by to back Pink up.

Making it so that only a few teams had any special right to force spoiled the design goal of everyone's equal.

Response & punishment are done by different teams: response by silver & its minions; punishment by Yellow through regenerative justice and therapies.
