## Rules Interpretation

![Magistrate|607](../../../images/team/gray/magistrate.svg)
Kids, none of which know the true nature of the game: how land is allocated & substances procured.

There are 13 different paths through childhood to a fully fledged member of the game. All of them involve some intense meetings with psychadelics in the company of their peers.
